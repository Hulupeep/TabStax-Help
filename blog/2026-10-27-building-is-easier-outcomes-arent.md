# Building is easier. Outcomes aren't.

AI makes writing code, producing plans, generating documents and creating tasks dramatically easier. But a mountain of output doesn't mean we've moved towards the result.

I spent years coding and years in product management. I love making things. Yet what I actually need is to build useful products, earn customers, get paid and sustain my family.

A thousand PRs are not one paying customer. Five hundred outreach emails are not five new customers.

The hard part is often identifying a real need, establishing trust and creating a commercial relationship.

That is why HeyStax is moving from preserving work context towards preserving **the intention behind the work**, coordinating humans and agents, and confronting the evidence of what happened.

For a research team, the equivalent is evidence and regulatory outcomes rather than document volume. For a builder, it is a completed construction milestone rather than another quote.

The question isn't just *how much can we produce?*

**What can we make happen?**
