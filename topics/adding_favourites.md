# <img src="../icons/tabstax-32.png" alt="TabStax" width="32" height="32" style="vertical-align: middle; margin-right: 10px;">Pin the stax that matter now

A favourite puts a stax at the top of the TabStax list, and up to three show as pills you can click.

## Why it helps

Billing week. A launch sprint. You switch between the same three stax all day. Pills keep them one click away, so you stop scrolling a long list.

## Favourite a stax

1. Open the **My Stax** dropdown.
2. Click the stax to expand it.
3. Click the **star** in its header bar.

<img src="../screenshots/fave_header.png" alt="Star icon in the stax header bar" width="400" style="display: block; margin: 20px auto;">

The stax now shows as a **pill** above your list.

## What you get

| | |
|---|---|
| Pills | Up to 3 above the list. Click one to open that stax. |
| Favourites | As many as you like, starred at the top of the dropdown. |

<img src="../screenshots/fave_list.png" alt="Favourite pills above the stax list" width="400" style="display: block; margin: 20px auto;">

## Unfavourite

1. Click the stax in the dropdown to expand it.
2. Click the **star** again.

The pill goes, and the stax drops back to its usual spot.

Now you can keep this week's work one click away, and swap it out when the week ends.
