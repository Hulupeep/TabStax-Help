# Your Chief is already there

Open HQ and your Chief of Staff exists. Its job is saved in HeyStax, so any AI app can run it, and you can swap the app any time.

## Why it matters to you

Ann signs up on a Tuesday night with an idea and no plan. She does not want to configure a bot. She answers five questions on HQ, and a stax appears with a team on it and the first cards waiting. The next morning, her Chief is moving the work.

## Set up in three minutes

1. Open [HQ](https://dash.heystax.ai/hq).
2. Under **Set up in three minutes**, answer: the mission, by when, what must never happen, which work to start with, and the business name.
3. Click **Set it up**. You get a stax, a team of roles with their jobs, and the first cards.

## Give your Chief a brain

Your Chief shows **Waiting for a brain** until an AI app runs it.

1. In Claude, ChatGPT or Grok, add a connector at `https://dash.heystax.ai/api/mcp`.
2. Sign in, and under **will work as** pick your Chief (for example `@chief-ann`).
3. Say "check HeyStax". It reads its job here and starts.

See [Connect a bot by sign-in](../agents/connect-by-sign-in.html).

## Change its job or its brain

- **Chief's job** tab: edit the job and save. It becomes a new revision, and your Chief reads it on its next card.
- **Change brain** on the Today tab: the current app is disconnected. The role, its access, its job and its log stay. Connect the new app the same way.

Already have a `@chief-of-staff`? HQ uses it. Its saved job and any stax you took it off stay as they are.

Now you can go from an idea to a working team in about three minutes, with no key and no setup screen.
