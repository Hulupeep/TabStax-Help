# Have your Chief make skills

You say which role needs to get better at what. Your Chief picks the kind of skill, writes it in your account and gives it to the role. You never run a script.

## Why it matters to you

Monday, 09:10. You correct the CFO's draft for the third time this month, the same way. That correction is a skill waiting to be written.

You should not be the one writing it. You give the direction. The Chief spots the pattern, gathers the proof and gets it built. Swap the brain in the seat next month and the method stays.

## The kind decides the route

| Kind | Triggered by | Where it ends up |
|---|---|---|
| **House** | A new standing rule of yours | Your house skill, which every role loads first |
| **Craft** | A role tripping on the same step twice | Your skills, given to that role. It can point at a file in your own repo |
| **Expertise** | "Give the CFO a tax method", or repeated corrections | Your skills, often copied from the library, given to the role |
| **Context** | Figures, people, deadlines | Cards, notes and dates on your stax. Never a skill |

More on each kind: [Keep method in skills, facts on the stax](../agents/four-kinds-of-skill). Your skills are on [dash.heystax.ai/skills](https://dash.heystax.ai/skills).

## How it works

1. **You ask, or the Chief spots it.** One line on a card, or the same correction twice on a role's cards.
2. **The Chief picks the kind.** Facts go straight onto the stax as cards.
3. **It checks the library first.** If a proven skill fits, it gives that to the role. You get your own copy.
4. **Or it writes one.** Each rule gets a "Why:" from a real card. HeyStax refuses a skill with facts in it, so nothing private leaks into a method.
5. **It gives the skill to the role.** The role's next card carries it. A fix is a new version, and the Chief moves the role to it.
6. **New roles get skills at birth.** When the Chief creates a role on a stax, it gives the role its skills in the same step.
7. **The Chief checks it helps.** Every card says which skill ran. If a skill does not help, it gets smaller or goes.
