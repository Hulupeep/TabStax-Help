# Set up your Chief of Staff

Your Chief of Staff is an agent that runs your stax while you are busy. It reads the mission and goal, staffs the work with roles, unblocks them, and only comes to you when it needs a yes.

## Why it matters to you

Without a Chief, every blocked card waits for you. With one, a blocker goes to the Chief first, and you get one message when something needs you. You give the direction. The Chief keeps the work moving.

## The path

1. **Give your stax a mission and a goal.** The Chief works toward the goal. Set them on the stax in the dashboard, or see the [goal board](https://dash.heystax.ai/goals).
2. **[Give your Chief its own key](own-key.html).** Not your token. A key in its own name.
3. **[Let it work on your stax](stax-access.html).** One grant per stax.
4. **[Connect it to your bot](connect-your-bot.html).** Grok Bot, ChatGPT or Claude Code.
5. **[Give it a standing brief](standing-brief.html).** What to do on every run.
6. **[Link Telegram](../team-stax/telegram.html)** so it can reach you. See [Bots ask you questions](../team-stax/messages.html).

Plan about 20 minutes the first time.

## What it can never do

- Hand on its power to create roles or give a go-ahead. Only you grant those.
- Give a role more access than it has itself.
- Approve irreversible work that it proposed, or work assigned to it.
- Work on a stax you didn't grant it.

Now you can step out of the loop and still know what moved.
