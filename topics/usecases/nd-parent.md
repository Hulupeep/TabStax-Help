# School admin for two parents, not one head

[← Back to Use Cases](index.md)

Niamh and Dev have a son, Rory, in second class. A trip to pay for by Friday. Tracksuit bottoms. A parent-teacher slot to book. It comes by app, email and a note in the school bag, and it all lands in Niamh's head.

## The stax

Niamh keeps one stax, **Rory: school this term**, shared with Dev.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Mission** | Rory has what he needs, and the load is shared. |
| **Goal** | Nothing for school is paid or bought on the morning it is due. |
| **Next Action** | One card per thing, with a name and a date. |

## What happens

1. **Save the tabs.** With the TabStax extension Niamh saves the school portal, the payment page and the term calendar as the stax.
2. **Catch it when it lands.** The trip note comes home. In their Telegram group she types `/t @dev pay for the class trip by Friday`. It lands on Dev's column.
3. **Close it from the phone.** Dev pays on his lunch break and replies `/done`.
4. **See the week.** Each morning at 08:00 the HeyStax Telegram bot sends the open list.
5. **Ask before the big day.** Before the school play, Niamh asks Claude, connected to HeyStax, "what is still open for Rory?" It answers from the cards.

## After

Either parent can see what is open and who has it. Nobody sweeps old emails at 07:45.

**It is yours.** The term's admin sits in your account, not in one parent's memory.

## What works today

| Step | State |
|---|---|
| Save and reopen the tabs with the TabStax extension; share the stax | live |
| `/t @handle`, reply `/done` and the 8am list in Telegram | live |
| Ask Claude or ChatGPT about the stax over MCP | live with your token; Claude.ai on the web is partial |

Now you can hand half the school admin to the other parent, by name.
