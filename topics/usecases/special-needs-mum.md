# Aoife and the call from the school

[← Back to Use Cases](index.md)

Aoife is at the kitchen table, finally getting some work done, when the school rings. Her son Darragh had a hard morning in class. They want to meet tomorrow.

His world already spans the school portal, his support plan, emails from the SENCO and the therapist, the benefits portal and her notes from past meetings. Every call used to mean a fresh scramble through all of it.

## The stax

Aoife keeps one stax, **Darragh: School and Supports**, and shares it with her partner Rob.

| | **Darragh: School and Supports** |
|---|---|
| **Mission** | Darragh gets the support he was promised, and we walk in with the full record. |
| **Goal** | The sensory corner and shorter written tasks confirmed in place by the end of term. |
| **Next Action** | Ask the SENCO what has been put in place since the last meeting. |

## What happens

1. **One click, everything open.** The TabStax extension reopens the portal, the support plan, the SENCO thread and her meeting notes.
2. **The meeting on the stax.** She adds tomorrow, 09:30, as a date.
3. **Today's one card.** "Write down what happened this morning" goes in the Must lane.
4. **Rob takes his share.** He sees the same stax and adds "Bring the therapist's last report" for himself.
5. **From the school gate.** On her phone she sends `/status` to the HeyStax Telegram bot and sees what is still open.

## After

She walks in as the person with the full record, not the parent pulled from something else. Rob read the same notes, not a garbled recap.

**It is yours.** What the school agreed sits in a stax she and Rob both see, not in one inbox.

## What works today

| Step | State |
|---|---|
| Save and reopen the tabs with the TabStax extension; share the stax | live |
| Attention lanes (Must, Should, Good, Meh) | live |
| Goals and dates on a stax | live |
| `/status` in Telegram | live |

Now you can take the school's call without the scramble.
