# Niamh switches rooms three times in one afternoon

[← Back to Use Cases](index.md)

Niamh runs a remote team. This afternoon she has a second call with an investor at 14:00, a final interview at 15:30 and the roadmap review at 17:00.

Each room wants a different story from the same company. Last month she opened the old roadmap in front of the investor.

## The stax

Niamh keeps one stax per room.

| Stax | Mission | Goal | Next Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seed round** | Fund the next 18 months | A second meeting with the partner is booked | Lead with why now and traction |
| **Senior engineer hire** | Hire someone who can own the backend | The panel agrees a yes or no today | Read the scorecard before 15:30 |
| **Q3 roadmap** | Pick what we will not build | Three priorities agreed by the team | Bring the trade-offs, not the backlog |

## What happens

1. **Save the tabs.** With the TabStax extension each stax keeps its own tabs: the deck and metrics, the CV and scorecard, the roadmap doc and retro notes.
2. **Share each one with the right people.** Her co-founder Tomás is on Seed round. The panel is on the hire. The team is on the roadmap.
3. **Switch rooms.** At 15:25 she closes Seed round and opens the hire. Only those tabs, only that next action.
4. **Hand on from the phone.** After the pitch she types `/t @tomas send the metrics pack` in Telegram. It lands as a card with his name on it.
5. **Ask without scrolling.** Before 17:00 she asks Claude, connected to HeyStax, "what is open on Q3 roadmap?" It answers from the stax.

## After

Niamh walks into each room with one story in her head, not three. The other two are parked in their stax, next action and all.

**It travels.** The same next actions show in Chrome, in Telegram and in Claude.

## What works today

| Step | State |
|---|---|
| Save and reopen a stax's tabs with the TabStax extension; share the stax | live |
| `/t @handle` in Telegram adds a card for that person | live |
| Ask Claude or ChatGPT about the stax over MCP | live with your token; Claude.ai on the web is partial |

Now you can change rooms without carrying the last one in with you.
