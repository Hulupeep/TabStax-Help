# Start from a template {% include badge-planned.html %}

A template will be a stax that is already staffed: its roles and their briefs, the mission and goal, shared tabs and standing cards. You copy it, add your people, give the roles their keys, and the work starts.

## Why it matters to you

Setting up a business development team of bots by hand takes an afternoon of prompts. A template will take a few minutes. What it never copies: people, keys, workers or contacts. Those stay yours to add.

> **Coming next.** Starting a stax from a template will be one step: pick the template, name the stax. It is tracked as tabstax#1085. Until then, build the same stax by hand. The steps below work today.
