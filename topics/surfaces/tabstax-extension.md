# Save a window, reopen it tomorrow

TabStax is the Chrome extension for HeyStax. It saves the tabs in a window as a stax, and reopens them in one click.

## Why it matters to you

Six tabs, two docs, a board and a thread. You have to leave. Tomorrow you will not remember which tabs, or what you were about to do.

Save them as a stax with a next action. When you come back, every tab opens and the next action is waiting. Pick up where your mind left off.

## How to do it

1. [Install TabStax from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/heystax/gfdcobncoohhlhppmoidalociahfdcam).
2. Click the TabStax icon in your toolbar.
3. Name the stax. Add `#tags` if you like: `cardano billing #clients`.
4. Add the next action.
5. Close the window. Reopen the stax from the icon when you are ready.

## What it saves

| Part | What you get |
|---|---|
| Tabs | Every tab in the window, reopened in one click |
| Next actions | The steps for this stax, shown when you open it |
| Tags | `#tag` in the name, to group stax by client or theme |
| Priority | Must, Should, Good or Meh, so you know which stax to open first |

## The same stax everywhere

The stax lives in your HeyStax account, not in your browser. A next action you add in the popup shows on the dashboard, in the `hey` CLI and in any AI app connected over MCP. The tabs are how you get back in. The next action is why.

Now you can close a window without losing the thought.
