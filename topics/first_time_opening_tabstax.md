# <img src="../icons/tabstax-32.png" alt="TabStax" width="32" height="32" style="vertical-align: middle; margin-right: 10px;">Save your first window as a stax

The first time you click TabStax, it offers to save the tabs in front of you as your first stax.

## Why it matters to you

Those tabs are not random. They are the thing you are doing right now. Save them with a name, close the window, and the work waits for you.

## How to do it

1. **Pin TabStax** to the toolbar, then click the icon.
2. The popup says **"No stax yet. Let's create your first one!"** and lists your open tabs.
3. **Name it** in the box at the bottom, such as `Q1 report #clients`. Words after `#` are optional tags.
4. Click **New**. TabStax saves the tabs and opens a **Start Page**.
5. Add a **next action** on the Start Page: what you do first when you come back.

## After that

| You | What happens |
|---|---|
| Add or close tabs while you work | The stax keeps up |
| Close the window | The stax is saved |
| Open the popup, find the stax in **My Stax**, click **Open All** | Every tab comes back, with your next actions on top |

Now you can close the whole window and pick up where your mind left off.
