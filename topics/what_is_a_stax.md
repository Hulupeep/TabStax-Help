# What is a stax?

A **stax** is one named piece of work: why you are doing it, what done looks like, and who does what next. People and agents work it side by side.

## Why it matters to you

You open a piece of work after two weeks away. No scrolling back through a chat. The stax says where you were and the one thing to do next.

## What's inside

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Mission** | Why this work exists |
| **Goal** | The result you can see when it is done |
| **Next actions** | Who does what next. Each one is a single step, with an owner |
| **People and agents** | Everyone on it, by `@handle`. Every change says who made it |
| **Tabs and links** | The pages for this work. The TabStax extension opens them in one click |
| **Breadcrumbs** | A trail of what happened, so coming back feels like a pause |
| **Priority** | Must, Should, Good or Meh: where it sits in your attention |

## Not a bookmark folder

A bookmark says: here is a link you saved once. A stax says: here is where you were, and here is what's next.

## The same stax everywhere

1. Add a next action from your **terminal**: `hey "review the PR"`.
2. Ask **Claude**: "mark the first action on API Migration as done".
3. Open the tabs from the **TabStax extension**.
4. Check the priority on the **dashboard**.

One stax, read and written by every surface. Nothing to copy across.

## If someone shared a stax with you

You get their working context: the same tabs, the same next actions. They can assign you a next action by `@handle`.

Now you can step back into any piece of work and know what to do next.
